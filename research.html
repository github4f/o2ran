Research

NTN-ready Open RAN, L/S-band satellite radio hardware, predictive beamforming, intelligent control and distributed edge computing.

02 / SCIENTIFIC PROGRAM

Research Thrusts

Three coordinated thrusts take the work from open architecture to integrated system-level validation.

THRUST 01

System Architecture & O-RAN for NTN

Design L/S-band satellite radio hardware and fronthaul, predictive/cooperative beamforming, NTN-aware RU/DU/CU splits, and RIC control for orbital delay, Doppler and mobility.

THRUST 02

Communication-Aware Distributed Edge Intelligence

Develop compute-aware service models, Near-RT xApps and Non-RT rApps for predictive slicing, workload placement and edge offloading.

THRUST 03

End-to-End Use Case Integration

Integrate the full NTN O-RAN stack across mission-driven scenarios, validate KPIs and demonstrate interoperability across heterogeneous nodes.

THRUST 1

Thrust 1 — System architecture and O-RAN for NTN

Foundation of the interoperable space–air–ground RAN, including the physical satellite radio hardware and its flight-integration pathway.

↑ Work packages
SATELLITE HARDWARE & FLIGHT PATHWAY

L/S-band radio development for satellite and CubeSat-class payloads

The project includes NTN RU hardware and fronthaul, Doppler mitigation, low-SWaP RF implementation, satellite electrical/mechanical interfaces, and space-environment packaging considerations such as thermal management, structural integrity and radiation/EMI shielding. A reproducible CubeSat-class design and payload integration pathway will support potential flight deployment. Any launch depends on mission qualification, safety review and launch availability; flat-sat and laboratory validation provide fallback paths.

T1.1Architecture & Functional SplitsSpecify how RU, DU, CU, RIC and SMO operate in NTN environments with Doppler, high RTT and mobility.M1–M6+

Planned subtasks

  1. T1.1.1M1–M2O²-RAN architecture, functions and NTN-aware split definitions.
  2. T1.1.2M2–M4F1, E2, A1, O1 and O2 interface adaptations and interoperability considerations.
  3. T1.1.3M3–M5DU/CU/RIC NTN features: timing advance, random access, HARQ, handover, QoS and xApps/rApps.
  4. T1.1.4M4–M6Integration framework, orbital/TLE data and latency, reliability and scalability KPIs.
T1.2L/S-Band Radio Unit, Fronthaul & Satellite Payload HardwareDesign and prototype L/S-band RUs and fronthaul for UAV and satellite payloads, including Doppler compensation, SWaP, satellite mechanical/electrical interfaces and space-environment design.M3–M30+

Planned subtasks

  1. T1.2.1M3–M8Satellite RU specifications, electrical/physical interface and payload packaging requirements.
  2. T1.2.2M9–M14Radio hardware, mechanical satellite interface prototypes, design and production reviews.
  3. T1.2.3M15–M20Submodule testing and laboratory risk reduction against system and environmental requirements.
  4. T1.2.4M21–M26Field testing and evaluation of prototypes.
  5. T1.2.5M26–M30Final testing and conversion into a sustained NTN test platform.
T1.3Predictive & Cooperative BeamformingDesign beamforming for direct LEO-to-user connectivity and multi-node satellite/UAV cooperation under Doppler, mobility and power constraints.M6–M30+

Planned subtasks

  1. T1.3.1M6–M12Beamforming specifications and system design document (SDD).
  2. T1.3.2M13–M18Implementation and integration of approved beamforming changes in the O-RAN RU/CU/DU stack.
  3. T1.3.3M19–M24Integration testing, including flight-test activities.
  4. T1.3.4M25–M30Flight testing, documentation and publication of systems and results.
T1.4DU/CU Design & IntegrationAdapt srsRAN-based DU/CU functions and placement, including mobility procedures, learning-based handover and power control.M4–M30+

Planned subtasks

  1. T1.4.1M4–M12DU/CU placement, functional splits and intra-/inter-DU/CU handover baselines.
  2. T1.4.2M10–M22Intelligent handover decision mechanisms and closed-loop power control.
  3. T1.4.3M20–M30DU/CU integration with RIC and end-to-end system validation.
T1.5RIC Architecture & Closed-Loop ControlImplement NTN-adapted Near-RT/Non-RT RIC functions and stable intelligent control loops.M14–M30+

Planned subtasks

  3. T1.5.3M18–M26rApps: orbital mobility planning, slice policy and UAV operations.
  4. T1.5.4M22–M30Cross-layer closed-loop integration and use-case validation.
