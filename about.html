About O2-RAN

Building a foundation for intelligent, resilient non-terrestrial communication infrastructure.

Extending Open RAN beyond terrestrial networks

Non-terrestrial networks (NTNs) are becoming essential to next-generation connectivity, but traditional network designs depend heavily on proprietary architectures and communication-only control. Satellites and uncrewed aerial vehicles (UAVs) present new challenges, including orbital mobility, Doppler shifts, intermittent links and strict size, weight and power constraints.

O2-RAN develops an AI-native, compute-aware Open RAN framework tailored to those conditions. The project adapts radio, distributed and central functions, extends RAN intelligent control and coordinates edge workloads across space, air and ground.

The research combines standards-aligned architecture, intelligent orchestration and experimental validation through three representative use cases: resilient aerial base stations, direct-to-cell connectivity and UAV swarms with multi-access edge computing.

PROJECT GOALS

Scientific objectives

Project goals based on the accepted O²-RAN research proposal.

01

NTN-ready disaggregated RAN

Design L/S-band NTN radio hardware and flight-payload interfaces alongside functional splits and RIC placement for satellite/aerial mobility, variable delays and backhaul.

02

AI-native predictive control

Develop predictive/cooperative beamforming and xApps/rApps for mobility forecasting, intelligent scheduling, network slicing and life-cycle-aware adaptation.

03

Joint communication & computing

Expose compute, queue, energy and workload state for coordinating services across diverse edge nodes.

04

Mission-oriented validation

Integrate and assess the architecture in resilient aerial networking, direct-to-cell satellite access and UAV swarm scenarios.

BROADER IMPACTS

Research, education & translation

The project is intended to strengthen resilience in disasters and other infrastructure-limited environments, foster workforce training in intelligent communications and aerospace systems, and support reproducible research through future releases of software, results and datasets.

Translation and demonstration partners contribute radio, UAV and satellite platforms, supporting the project’s path from research prototypes toward larger-scale technology validation. These are research objectives; achieved impacts and performance will be reported after evaluation.
