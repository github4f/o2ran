01 / PROJECT OVERVIEW

About O2-RAN

An open foundation for 5G/6G satellite networks and space-based edge intelligence.

5G connectivity and intelligent computing beyond Earth

O2-RAN (Orbital Open RAN for Non-Terrestrial Networks) aims to establish a foundation for 5G satellite communications, direct-to-cell connectivity, in-orbit edge computing and space-based data center capabilities. The project develops an AI-native, compute-aware Open Radio Access Network (O-RAN) framework tailored to non-terrestrial networks (NTNs).

A central objective is to design, build and launch an O2-RAN CubeSat into low Earth orbit (LEO). The planned satellite will provide an experimental platform for end-to-end development and on-orbit validation of 5G/O-RAN satellite networks, integrating satellite communications and onboard computing with intelligent RAN controllers and terrestrial 5G infrastructure.

To address Doppler shifts, propagation delays, dynamic satellite topology and constrained onboard resources, O2-RAN will investigate adaptive RAN functional splits, intelligent placement of communication and computing workloads, and AI-driven orchestration across satellites, unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs) and ground infrastructure.

A key innovation is advancing in-orbit edge computing and the foundations of space-based data center services for AI inference, data processing and distributed computing in space. The platform will be validated through three use cases: (1) LEO satellite direct-to-cell connectivity; (2) UAV-enabled aerial O-RAN for disaster-resilient communications; and (3) UAV swarms offloading AI and computing workloads to satellites. Together, the CubeSat mission, hardware experiments and on-orbit demonstrations aim to establish a reproducible foundation for resilient 5G/6G space–air–ground networks.

PROJECT GOALS

Scientific objectives

From NTN-adapted Open RAN to a CubeSat-enabled, in-orbit computing testbed.

01

NTN-ready 5G/Open RAN

Develop L/S-band radio hardware, adaptable RU/DU/CU functional splits and AI-native RIC control for LEO mobility, Doppler, variable delay and terrestrial 5G integration.

02

O2-RAN CubeSat mission

Design, build and launch a dedicated CubeSat to support end-to-end 5G/O-RAN prototyping, satellite payload integration and on-orbit experimentation.

03

In-orbit edge computing

Explore onboard AI inference, data processing, workload placement and satellite-hosted computing as foundations for space-based data center capabilities.

04

End-to-end use-case validation

Evaluate direct-to-cell LEO access, disaster-resilient aerial O-RAN and UAV swarms offloading AI workloads to satellites through lab and orbital demonstrations.

BROADER IMPACTS

Research, education & translation

O2-RAN aims to broaden 5G/6G connectivity and enable intelligent computing beyond Earth, including direct-to-cell services, resilient aerial communications and distributed AI processing in orbit. The planned CubeSat mission will create hands-on opportunities for interdisciplinary research and workforce training across wireless communications, aerospace systems and edge computing.

The project seeks to release reproducible research tools, software and experimental findings while advancing hardware prototypes, satellite deployment and on-orbit validation with academic and industry collaborators. CubeSat launch, space-based data center capabilities and system performance are planned research objectives, not completed achievements.
