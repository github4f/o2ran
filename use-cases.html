Application Use Cases

Representative scenarios guide technical development and validate the O²-RAN architecture.

UC 01

Disaster-Resilient Aerial Base Stations

UAV-mounted base stations restore communications in areas affected by damaged or unavailable ground infrastructure.

  • Rapidly deployable aerial connectivity
  • Resilient backhaul and adaptive network control
  • Service prioritization for emergency operations
UC 02

Direct-to-Cell Satellite & CubeSat Connectivity

CubeSat-class L/S-band radio payloads and regenerative NTN functions support a planned direct-to-cell validation pathway, building toward satellite links with user devices.

  • CubeSat direct-to-cell payload and testbed
  • Predictive beam, Doppler and handover management
  • NTN-aware RU/DU/CU integration
UC 03

UAV Swarms with MEC Offloading

Coordinated UAVs distribute sensing and AI workloads across aerial, satellite and ground edge resources.

  • Collaborative sensing and inference
  • Compute-communication co-optimization
  • Predictive workload migration
INTEGRATED VALIDATION

How use cases drive the research

UC 01

Disaster-resilient aerial base stations

Investigates UAV-mounted RAN operation with limited and variable satellite backhaul, adaptive functional splits, first-responder traffic prioritization and predictive handover. Primarily exercised through the planned M23 hardware demonstration and M30 UAV field demonstration.

UC 02

CubeSat direct-to-cell connectivity & validation

UC2 includes a CubeSat-class direct-to-cell demonstration pathway—not only generic satellite connectivity. The team is developing L/S-band satellite radio hardware, CubeSat payload interfaces, and onboard RU/DU/CU software integration, together with Doppler/timing compensation, predictive beamforming and direct links to user devices.

Validation progresses through laboratory, flat-sat and hardware-in-the-loop platforms alongside the M11, M18 and M23 NTN demonstrations. CubeSat flight or on-orbit experiments depend on hardware qualification and a confirmed launch opportunity.

Explore CubeSat payload engineering

UC 03

Distributed UAV swarm edge intelligence

Explores intelligent workload placement and migration among UAVs and MEC platforms, combining sensing, AI inference, and networking under resource limits. Validated through the planned M30 and M36 integrated demonstrations.
